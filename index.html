FAQ

Quick answers

Who is Syed Mateen?

Syed Mateen, also written Syyed Mateen (full name Syed Abdul Mateen), is an AI automation and digital marketing specialist from Pakistan. He builds AI voice agents and n8n workflows, runs Meta and Google ad campaigns and creates CGI product ads and video edits for businesses in the US and worldwide.

What services does Syed Mateen offer?

Three things. AI automation is the main one: voice agents, chat agents and n8n workflows that answer, book and follow up. Then paid ads on Meta and Google built around leads. And CGI product ads with video editing for Reels, Shorts and TikTok.

What is an AI voice booking agent?

A phone agent that answers calls, collects the caller's details and books the appointment. Syed's clinic version checks opening hours and Google Calendar, offers the nearest free slot, blocks double bookings, logs the patient in Google Sheets and emails a confirmation.

Can you make a product ad without a film shoot?

Yes. The scenes are created digitally around your product, then edited, scored and captioned for Reels, Shorts and TikTok. Clear photos of the product are enough to start.

Which tools do you work with?

n8n, ElevenLabs, HubSpot, Google Calendar, Sheets and Gmail, Slack, Telegram, Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads and CapCut.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. Most of the work is remote, with businesses in the United States and worldwide.

How long does it take to build an AI agent?

A focused agent such as a booking line or a lead follow up flow usually takes one to two weeks, including testing with your real calendar, CRM and inbox.
