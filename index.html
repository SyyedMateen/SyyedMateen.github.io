FAQ

Quick answers.

What does Syed Mateen do?

Syed Mateen builds AI agents and automations for businesses (voice and chat agents, booking and onboarding workflows, content pipelines), makes CGI product ads with AI video tools, and runs Meta and Google ad campaigns. He works with Lenaar Digital on AI automation and social media marketing for US clients.

What is an AI voice booking agent?

A phone agent that answers calls, collects the caller's details and books the appointment. Syed's clinic version uses ElevenLabs for the voice and n8n for the logic. It checks opening hours and Google Calendar, offers the nearest free slot, blocks double bookings, logs the patient in Google Sheets and emails a confirmation.

Can you make a product ad without a film shoot?

Yes. CGI product ads are made with AI video tools, then edited and captioned for Reels, Shorts and TikTok.

Which tools do you use?

n8n, ElevenLabs, ChatGPT and Claude, Google Calendar, Sheets and Gmail, Klap, Telegram, Slack, HubSpot, Meta Ads and Google Ads.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. Mostly remote work with businesses in the United States and worldwide.
