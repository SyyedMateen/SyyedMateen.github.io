FAQ

Quick answers

Do you run Meta and Google ads?

Yes. Campaign setup, audiences, creative testing and conversion tracking on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google (Search and YouTube).

Can you make the ad creative too?

Yes. CGI product ads and short videos are made in house so the creative and the campaign are planned together.

How do you measure results?

By cost per lead and booked calls. Conversion tracking goes in before the first dollar is spent.

Why hire a Facebook ads expert instead of running ads yourself?

Whether you call it an FB ads specialist or a Meta ads expert, the job is the same. Most self-run accounts lose money in the same places: broken tracking, broad audiences and one creative running for months. A Facebook ads specialist fixes those first, then tests new creative every week and judges everything on cost per lead.

How do you lower cost per lead on Facebook ads?

Fix tracking first so Meta optimises for real leads, then test new hooks and creative every week, tighten the offer and the form, and follow up every lead within minutes. That last step often lowers the cost per booked call more than any targeting change.

How do you set up conversion tracking in Google Ads?

Each lead action, like a form submit, call or booking, gets its own conversion action, fired by Google tag or Google Tag Manager and checked against real leads in your CRM. Google Ads conversion tracking goes in before any money is spent.

Do you run Facebook ads for med spas and dentists?

Yes. Med spa marketing and dental practices are a strong fit: Facebook ads for med spas and dentists bring in consultation requests, Google ads for dentists capture people searching right now, and an AI receptionist books them.

Are you a lead generation agency?

Not a big lead generation agency. You work with one PPC specialist who runs the ads, makes the creative and connects the follow up, which is often a better fit for lead generation for small business. Facebook lead ads, Google Search and YouTube are the main channels.

Should I hire a Google Ads consultant or an agency?

A consultant suits a small or medium account that needs one person who knows it inside out and answers quickly. An agency suits big budgets spread over many channels and markets. If your goal is leads from Meta and Google with fast follow up, one specialist usually moves faster.

Do you handle Meta Conversions API setup and Facebook pixel setup?

Yes. The Meta pixel and the Conversions API (Meta CAPI) are set up together as a server side tracking setup so leads are still counted when browsers block tracking, and Google Ads gets its own conversion tags. Results are checked against your CRM so the numbers match real leads.

What happens to a lead after it comes in?

It goes straight into your CRM or Google Sheet, gets an instant reply and, if you want, a call from an AI voice agent within minutes. Fast follow up is often the cheapest way to lower cost per booked call.
