Case 03 · CGI ad · Spec work

Selling hydration without saying "hydration."

Twenty seconds. Not one word of copy. And you still know what the serum does.

In short

A vertical CGI spec ad for Pond's Hydra Miracle, made with AI video tools. The whole frame stays in the bottle's pale blue, water tells the story, lab glassware signals science, and a corridor of bottles narrows to one.

Type
Spec ad
Length
About 20 seconds
Format
9:16 for Reels and Shorts
First shared

The brief

A self-initiated spec ad. Pond's and Hydra Miracle are trademarks of Unilever.

The brief: sell hydration without saying "hydration." No voiceover, no on-screen copy, built for Reels and Shorts.

The decisions

One colour, start to finish

Everything lives in the brand's pale blue, so the product never fights for attention.

Water does the talking

Drops, ripples and a bottle sinking into water make the connection before anyone reads the label.

Lab shots for trust

Beakers in the background say "formulated," not "decorated."

Repetition, then focus

A corridor of bottles shows scale, then the camera narrows to one.

The first second decides

On Reels, people decide in about a second. The opening shot has to earn the rest.

The takeaway

Most beauty ads try to say five things. This one tries to make you feel one.
