FAQ

Quick answers.

What is a CGI ad?

A product ad where the scenes are generated rather than filmed: liquids, splashes, exploding layers and impossible camera moves that would cost a studio day to shoot.

Do I need to send the product?

No. Clear photos of the product and its label are enough to build the shots around it.

Which formats do I get?

Vertical 9:16 for Reels, Shorts and TikTok by default, plus square or widescreen cuts when you need them.
