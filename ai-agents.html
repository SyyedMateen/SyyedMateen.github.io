FAQ

Quick answers.

What can an AI agent do for a small business?

Answer calls and messages, book appointments into your calendar, qualify leads, send follow-ups and keep your CRM or Google Sheets up to date, day and night.

Which tools do you build with?

n8n for the workflows, ElevenLabs for voice, Claude and ChatGPT models for the thinking, plus Google Calendar, Sheets, Gmail, Slack, Telegram and HubSpot.

How long does a build take?

A focused agent, like a booking line, usually takes one to two weeks including testing with your real tools.
